(01) — The brief

Two ageing roofs, fully renewed. New washrooms, built from shell.

Alpine MI delivered a combined refurbishment package for Rhubarb Hospitality, protecting the occupied office building with a complete flat-roof renewal while also constructing new washroom facilities from an empty shell.

The four-week programme brought together specialist waterproofing, new plumbing and drainage, electrical installations, ventilation, partitions and premium interior finishes within one coordinated main-contractor delivery.

(03) — Washroom constructionEmpty shell to turnkey

Boutique-hotel character, inside an office setting

The new washrooms were constructed entirely from an unused area. Alpine MI formed the partitions and openings, installed all hot and cold-water services, waste and drainage, then completed lighting, ventilation and power before delivering the final decorative scheme.

New partitionsNew plumbing and drainageLED lightingMechanical ventilationSage-green panellingPatterned wallpaper
(02) — Roof refurbishment700+ m²

Seamless protection, built for the long term

The building’s two ageing flat roofs—one felt and one asphalt—were cleaned, repaired and prepared before receiving substrate-specific reinforced liquid waterproofing systems. Upstands, gutters, drainage outlets and perimeter details were fully incorporated to eliminate vulnerable joints and weak points.
