(01) — The brief

Natural timber, restored to perfection. Ready for service.

We delivered a complete timber floor refurbishment that revitalised the restaurant’s luxury interior. The existing floor was carefully sanded back to bare timber before receiving a premium natural wood stain that enhanced the richness and character of the grain.

A durable commercial-grade protective coating completed the restoration, creating a warm, timeless finish designed to withstand the demands of a busy, high-end hospitality environment.
