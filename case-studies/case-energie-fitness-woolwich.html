Energie Fitness, Woolwich.

N° 19 — SE18 Project photography · 2026
(01) — The brief

A high-street fitness club, delivered as a complete environment.

Alpine MI delivered the fit-out of a new Energie Fitness club in Woolwich, transforming a high-street unit into a fully equipped, member-ready fitness destination. The 5,400 ft² scheme brings together cardio, strength, functional training and dedicated studio areas within one clear and durable layout.

The programme required close coordination of specialist flooring, exposed services, lighting, branded finishes and equipment zones. Every area was planned around member flow, intensive daily use and the operational requirements of a modern gym.
