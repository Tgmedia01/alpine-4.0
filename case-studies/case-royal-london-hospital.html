(01) — The brief

A modern dental department, built for clinical performance. Bright, hygienic and compliant.

Alpine MI delivered the complete fit-out of a new dental department within The Royal London Hospital, one of the UK’s busiest NHS hospitals. The project demanded meticulous planning, healthcare-approved materials and strict compliance throughout.

Treatment rooms, clinical support areas and circulation spaces were constructed and fitted out around the needs of healthcare professionals and patients, creating a durable and fully operational department.

(03) — M&E infrastructureIntegrated services

Specialist infrastructure, coordinated room by room

The complete mechanical and electrical installation included power, lighting, emergency systems, dental equipment supplies, hot and cold water, waste connections and specialist clinical sinks. Services were positioned for maximum operational efficiency while keeping rooms clear and uncluttered.
