(01) — The brief

A resident gym with atmosphere, identity and precision.

Alpine MI completed the fit-out of a 2,500 ft² gym for Vertus in Canary Wharf. Located within an office-led building, the space was developed as a premium amenity with a distinctive visual identity and a carefully balanced mix of cardio, strength and recovery areas.

The finished environment combines dark finishes, exposed services, integrated joinery and feature lighting. Each area has its own character while remaining part of one coherent and highly functional fitness space.
